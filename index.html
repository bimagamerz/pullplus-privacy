Privacy Policy — Pull+

Last updated: October 2026

Pull+ ("the App") is a pull-up training application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Pull+ does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily enter:

  • Name and gender — entered during onboarding, stored only on your device, used to personalize your profile display.
  • Training progress — sessions completed, streaks, and program progress, stored only locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or any server.

We do not operate a backend server or database. All app data is stored locally on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App or use the "Reset Progress" option in Settings.

2. Third-Party Services

Pull+ uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and general usage data to serve and measure ads. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.

3. Device Permissions

Pull+ may request the following permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

  • Notifications — to send you a daily training reminder, if you enable this feature. You may disable it anytime in Settings.
  • Calendar — to add recurring training events to your device's calendar app, if you choose to set a training schedule. You may deny this permission; the App will continue to function normally without it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as necessary for the AdMob advertising service described above, which operates under Google's own privacy practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data (profile, progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only. You can delete all app data at any time by using "Reset Progress" in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Pull+ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
